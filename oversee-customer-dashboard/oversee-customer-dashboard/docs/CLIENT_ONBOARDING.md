## What you'll see when you sign in

Sign in at **`/login/`** with the credentials you used at checkout. You'll land on **`/dashboard/`**, your private workspace.

The sidebar groups your tools into four sections:

- **MAIN** — Home, My Projects, Files
- **COMMUNICATION** — Messages, Schedule a call, Performance reports
- **RESOURCES** — Documents, Reviews, Knowledge base
- **COMMERCE** — Services, Billing, Account

## Your projects

Every Oversee service you've purchased becomes a **project board**. Boards have:

- **Groups** for each phase or month of work.
- **Items** — the actual tasks. Click an item to see its description, due date, and the activity feed.
- **Updates** — the chronological feed of progress. Your account manager posts here; you can reply, attach files, drop in a Loom recording, or pin important updates.

If your service is a monthly subscription, the board automatically rolls over each month — last month's group is archived, and a fresh group appears at the top.

## Talking to your team

- **Messages** — one inbox connecting your phone, SMS, email, and your account manager.
- **Schedule a call** — direct access to your account manager's calendar.
- **Performance reports** — live metrics for the work we're doing for you.

If any of those say *"isn't connected yet"*, message your account manager — we'll link your account in seconds.

## Billing and services

- **Services** — browse Oversee services and add new ones to your plan.
- **Billing** — view invoices, update your payment method, manage your subscription.
- **Account** — update your name, email, password, and notification preferences.

## Tips

- Use **⌘K** (or Ctrl+K on Windows) to search across everything.
- Toggle **dark mode** with the moon/sun icon in the top right — it sticks across sessions.
- Drag and drop files anywhere on a board to attach them.

Questions? Hit **Messages** — your account manager replies in the same dashboard.
