Conversation AI - Bot Inactive (Sleep) Conditions

In Conversation AI there are several different events that can put the bot in a conversation "to sleep".

TABLE OF CONTENTS

  • Bot Settings
  • Bot Goals
  • Conversation
  • Workflow

Bot Settings

  • Bot Settings > Max Messages - When the total messages in the conversation reaches this limit.


Bot Goals

  • Bot Goals > Stop Bot - When the condition in this goal is reached.

  • Bot Goals > Appointment Booking > Post Appointment Booked - after an appointment is successfully booked.

  • Bot Goals > Human Handover - When the goal is reached and the conversation calls in a human.

  • Bot Goals > Transfer Bot - When the condition is triggered to switch to a different bot.

Conversation

  • Conversation > Manual Input - When a human user sends a message in the conversation.


  • Conversation > Manual Switch to Inactive - In the Conversations panel, manually switch the Conversation AI from Active to Inactive.

Workflow


  • Workflow > Action - When a workflow action sets the conversation to inactive/sleep.
  • Workflow > Message Sent - When a workflow sends a message in the conversation.
