What is Auto Recharge and how does it work ?

 

  1. The amount you see in dropdown 1 is the amount that will be deducted from your payment method when your balance falls below the amount set in dropdown 2.
  2. Dropdown 2 is simply the threshold dropdown. the amount you set here will work as the threshold amount. so as soon as your wallet balance hits below this auto recharge will trigger with the amount set in dropdown 1.




Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


What is Auto Recharge, and how does it work?

Auto Recharge automatically replenishes your wallet balance once it falls below a set threshold. When your wallet balance goes below the threshold amount you've specified (dropdown 2), the recharge amount selected (dropdown 1) is deducted from your payment method to refill your wallet.
