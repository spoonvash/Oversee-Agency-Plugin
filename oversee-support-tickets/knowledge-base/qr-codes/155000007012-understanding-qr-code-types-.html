Understanding QR Code Types!

The QR Code Generator makes it easy to create scannable codes that drive users to specific actions such as opening a webpage, leaving a review, starting a call, or submitting a form.
This guide explains each QR type, what it’s best used for, and how to set it up.


1. Website

Purpose: Direct users to any webpage.
Use Cases:

  • Link to your booking, sales, or calendar page

  • Share instructions, menus, or resources on printed materials

  • Promote time-sensitive offers in-store or at events

Setup: Enter the full URL (e.g., https://yourdomain.com/book).

2. Review Link

Purpose: Send users straight to a review platform.
Use Cases:

  • Collect Google or Facebook reviews after a service

  • Add to receipts, counters, or signage to boost review volume

  • Use at events to gather real-time customer feedback

Setup: Paste the review link from your Review Request template or use a direct URL.

3. Call

Purpose: Start a phone call immediately when scanned.
Use Cases:

  • Add to storefronts for “Call Us Now” access

  • Use on print ads so customers can contact you instantly

  • Offer quick support without requiring a website visit

Setup: Enter your business phone number (e.g., +15551234567).

4. SMS

Purpose: Open a text message with a pre-filled number and optional message.
Use Cases:

  • Trigger automations using text keywords (e.g. “BOOK”)

  • Let customers text support instead of calling

  • Promote text-to-join campaigns or loyalty programs

Setup: Enter the phone number and optional message text.

5. Email

Purpose: Open an email draft with your preset address, subject, or message.
Use Cases:

  • Collect support inquiries

  • Let event attendees quickly reach your team

  • Provide a lightweight alternative to a form submission

Setup: Enter the recipient email and optional subject/message body.

6. Payment

Purpose: Direct customers to a payment or checkout link.
Use Cases:

  • Collect payments at events or pop-up locations

  • Add QR codes to invoices for faster processing

  • Accept donations or contributions onsite

Setup: Add a payment link, invoice link, or checkout URL.

7. WhatsApp

Purpose: Start a WhatsApp message with your business.
Use Cases:

  • Offer instant, global-friendly customer support

  • Let leads reach your team quickly without filling out a form

  • Add to brochures or posters for easy communication

Setup: Enter your WhatsApp business number in international format and an optional message.

8. Funnel

Purpose: Link to a specific Funnel or Funnel Step.
Use Cases:

  • Drive traffic to lead capture funnels

  • Promote offers at live events

  • Send users directly to order pages or upsells

Setup: Choose the funnel and step from your account.

9. Form

Purpose: Let users complete a Form immediately after scanning.
Use Cases:

  • Capture leads at trade shows, booths, or store counters

  • Collect walk-in feedback or contact information

  • Replace paper forms at events

Setup: Select the form you want users to access.

10. Survey

Purpose: Share a Survey to collect responses.
Use Cases:

  • Post-appointment satisfaction surveys

  • Collect event or workshop feedback

  • Measure customer experience across touchpoints

Setup: Select your survey from the available list.

11. Quiz

Purpose: Direct users to a Quiz.
Use Cases:

  • Lead qualification quizzes

  • Onboarding or product-match flows

  • Interactive experiences at events or in-store displays

Setup: Select the quiz from your account.

12. Profile Card

Purpose: Display a digital profile with personal details.
Use Cases:

  • Agents, reps, or staff sharing contact info at events

  • Use on badges or printed handouts

  • Provide quick access to your bio and key links

Setup: Fill in your profile information such as name, title, and contact details.

13. VCard

Purpose: Save your contact details directly to a user’s phone.
Use Cases:

  • Networking events

  • Digital alternative to physical business cards

  • Quick contact sharing for sales teams

Setup: Add your contact information including name, phone, email, and company.

14. Business Card

Purpose: Share both personal and company details in one digital card.
Use Cases:

  • Team members who need a unified contact card

  • Printed materials that link to full business info

  • Replace or complement traditional business cards

Setup: Enter both personal and business information.

Dynamic QR Codes

All QR types support dynamic URLs, allowing you to:

  • Update the destination without reprinting the code

  • Track scans and engagement data

  • Reuse one QR code for multiple promotions

This makes them ideal for long-term use in print materials, signage, packaging, and campaigns.
